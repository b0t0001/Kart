**About $12 before tax.**

**Don't buy garlic bread.** Kart puts it on the list, and leaving it out is what triggers *"Before you go: you still need garlic bread"* at checkout (beta).

**Optional, nothing to buy:** for the "Is this…?" trust moment, drop any item you already have that isn't in the store's catalog, like a water bottle.

**In the store:** photograph the **A12 end sign** and note which aisle the **bakery** is in (that's where Kart points for the garlic bread). **Keep the receipt.**
