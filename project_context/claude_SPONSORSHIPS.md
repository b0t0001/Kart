## Recommendation: go deep on Anthropic + Cartesia

Build a **voice-first physical device for one specific community**, with Claude as the brain and Cartesia as the voice. Same project, no extra scope, eligible for:

| Prize | Why we fit |
|---|---|
| **Anthropic** — $5k Claude credits + 1yr Max 20x + office hours | Rewards *a named community, a real problem, it works, thoughtful Claude use*. Hardware in a real setting is a strong "usable on Monday" story. |
| **Cartesia** — Meta glasses (TBD) | Voice has to be *core*, not added on. Their own idea list includes "ambient / wearable assistant — help without pulling out a phone." 25% of the score is accessibility/privacy, which lines up with Anthropic's "where it could go wrong." |
| **Best Hardware Hack** (general) — Bambu Lab A1 Mini | Comes free with a working physical build. |
| **Best People-First Hack** (general) — Apple Watch SE 3 | Comes free with a community-focused framing. |

**Why these two:** their judging criteria overlap almost completely: real users, a working demo over ambition, responsible design. One pitch covers both. The other strong tracks are either finance (Hyphenate, CRS, Capital One, Visa, Coinbase), software-only platforms that fit awkwardly on hardware, or not announced yet.

**Rules for both tracks**
- Name the community narrowly. "Elderly people" is too broad; "low-vision residents at a specific senior home" is the level they want. Talk to a real person in it (a call on Saturday counts).
- Cartesia README: setup, **which Cartesia features we used**, known limitations. No API keys in the repo.
- Cartesia pitch is demo-first: 2 min, one complete user flow, then problem + how we built it.
- Be ready to say where Claude could fail the user and what we did about it.
- Voice latency matters to Cartesia judges. Keep the voice loop tight: stream TTS, use short Claude responses.

**Alternate second track:** **Meta, "Bringing People Closer Together with AI."** Pick it if the idea is about connecting people rather than helping one person. The prize is a trip to a Round 2 hackathon at Menlo Park. It pairs well with Anthropic too.

**Zero-scope add-on (optional):** **Notability**. Just keep our sketches and notes in Notability from the start; judges score the *process*. Only do this if someone will actually keep it up. Notes added at the end score poorly.
